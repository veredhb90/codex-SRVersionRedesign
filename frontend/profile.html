WHY SHARE YOUR TRADES
📈Build a real track record
Post the actual trades from your real trading account with entry, target and stop. Every position is tracked live and scored win/loss automatically — a transparent, verifiable history of how you trade.
📊Improve your performance
Every posted trade is scored win/loss and rolled into your live stats. Over time you — and the AI — can see exactly what's working and what isn't: the setups, tickers and timing behind your results, so you sharpen your edge with real data instead of memory.
🧠Your 24/7 AI analyst
The SwingRush AI works around the clock on the trades you post — pulling fresh news, earnings and analyst reports in real time to give well-studied recommendations on each open position, on your account as a whole, and on the market in general. Ask it any time whether to hold, close or adjust.
👥See the crowd's positioning
For every ticker you trade, see how the wider SwingRush community is positioned — the share of traders going BUY vs SELL — so you know whether you're with the crowd or against it.
WHY YOUR REAL INPUTS MATTER
🎯The more real your inputs, the smarter your advice
Your age, capital, trading style, experience, risk tolerance and goals are exactly what the AI uses to tailor every recommendation to you. Accurate inputs mean position sizing, risk levels and trade ideas that actually fit your situation — vague or empty inputs only get generic advice. Keep them real and up to date to get the best guidance on your trades and your whole account.
